## A memory has a place

An idea for connecting photographs with places: explore a personal archive through location, time, and camera metadata.

## Questions to explore

- Can a map help rediscover photographs that a chronological gallery hides?
- Which metadata should remain private by default?
- How can location be useful without publishing precise personal coordinates?
